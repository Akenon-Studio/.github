# Security policy

Please report security problems privately. **Never open a public issue.**

Email **security@akenon-studio.com** with:

- what you found and where (product, version, URL or file)
- steps to reproduce
- what an attacker could do with it

We reply within 3 working days and keep you updated until it is fixed. Please give us a reasonable
time to fix it before telling anyone else.

This applies to the Akenon app, the website, the marketplace, the SDK and our devices.
